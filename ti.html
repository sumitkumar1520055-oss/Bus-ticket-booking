🛡️ PalBuses User Privacy & Data Protection Policy

🔒 1. Information Collection & Usage

We collect essential booking metadata including Contact Name, Mobile Number, Email Address, and Boarding/Dropping Preferences. This data is exclusively used to generate official e-Tickets, send SMS departure alerts, process cancellations, and verify passenger identity during boarding.

🛡️ 2. Data Confidentiality & Zero Telemarketing Guarantee

PalBuses enforces a strict Zero Spam Policy. We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or share your contact number or email address with third-party telemarketers or advertising networks.

💳 3. Payment Security & Encryption Standards

All online financial transactions are protected using PCI-DSS compliant gateways with 256-bit SSL encryption. PalBuses servers never store your credit card numbers, CVVs, net banking credentials, or UPI PINs.

🚌 4. Operator Manifest Sharing Protocol

Passenger name, seat assignment, and mobile number are shared solely with the specific bus operator assigned to your booking for passenger manifest verification and emergency trip coordination.

📜 5. User Data Rights (DPDP & GDPR Compliance)

Under Digital Personal Data Protection standards, you retain full rights to inspect, update, download, or request permanent deletion of your passenger data from our system records.
